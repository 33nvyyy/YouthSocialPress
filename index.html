Who we are

A platform for young voices.

The Youth Social Press is a youth-led journal dedicated to empowering young voices through journalism, research and critical engagement with politics, society and culture. We aim to foster informed discussion, encourage independent thought and provide young people with a platform to explore, question and contribute to the issues shaping our world.
